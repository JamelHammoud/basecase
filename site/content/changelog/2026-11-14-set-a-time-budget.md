title: Set a time budget for your robot
date: 2026-11-14

You can now give your robot a budget in hours beside its money budget. Checks compares the estimated printing, assembly, and servo setup time with it.

In the Build tab, enter hours in the Time field under Bill of materials. The Time check shows the three contributions together.

A rover for this weekend can fit your money budget while taking longer to print and assemble than you have available. The check fails above your hours and warns above 90 percent, so that tradeoff appears while you design.

Print hours are divided across your saved printers, with at least one counted. Sliced plates replace only their own estimates. Assembly uses guide steps, parts, fasteners, and inserts, while servo setup allows five minutes per movable joint.

Fix sends the longest plate, the step with the most fasteners, and the servo count to the agent. It can reduce print time or assembly work through edits you can undo.

These are planning estimates. Delivery, print failures, and print service lead times are excluded. Clearing the budget leaves the total as a note.

Steps are in [Set a time budget](/help/set-a-time-budget).
