**What I couldn't match:**
- **Body length:** about 10 mm over the 620 mm upper limit. The torso's rounded front and rear ends sit outside the bony points; shortening further would flatten the chest front and buttocks.
- **Tail:** in the relaxed straight-back pose the tip sits at about z 330 mm, well above the hock at about 150 mm. Its length would only reach the hock if it hung straight down.
- **Front view:** I found no free photo of a Lab standing square from the front. Chest width (260 mm) and leg spacing come from breed norms and a photo of a black Lab walking toward the camera.
- **Ears:** larger edge fillets kept timing out, so up close the flaps still have fairly crisp edges and read as slightly flat plates.
- **Show photo vs. standard:** the Narva dog has a heavier neck ruff and a longer body than the standard, mostly from coat and the stretched show stance. I followed the standard, so the model looks leaner than that photo through the neck and shoulders.
