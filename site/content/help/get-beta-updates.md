Get new versions of Basecase before they reach everyone, by switching your updates to the beta channel.

Beta updates are off until you turn them on. With them on, you still get every stable release too.

## Turn on beta updates

1. On a Mac, choose **Basecase** ▸ **Get Beta Updates**. On Windows, click **Menu**, then choose **Help** ▸ **Get Beta Updates**.

A check mark shows next to the item. Basecase checks for a beta right away, and downloads it if there's a newer one.

2. When the blue arrow appears above **Settings** in the view rail, click it to restart and install.

## Go back to stable releases

1. Choose **Get Beta Updates** again to clear the check mark.

Basecase never installs an older version over a newer one. So you keep the beta you have until a stable release with a higher version number ships.

Steps for installing any update are in [Update Basecase](/help/update-basecase).
