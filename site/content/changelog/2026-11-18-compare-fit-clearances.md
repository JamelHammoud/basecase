You can print several dimension values on a comparison plate in Print Fit Test, then apply your choice to the editable design.

For a 608 bearing seat, you can compare bore diameters of 21.9, 22 and 22.1 mm before you print the whole housing. Each coupon comes from regenerated host geometry and keeps its print orientation and material.

You can compare 2 to 8 distinct millimeter values. You can see failed candidates and the reasons they failed in the list. The ordinary 3MF export includes a source hash and an identity map linking each coupon to its dimension value.

Apply a value with one parameter edit, which you can undo and redo. If you change or delete the source, you can't apply the value. You can't select calculated expressions, angular dimensions or catalog dimensions. Choosing a value records a design choice, without claiming a physical measurement or printer calibration.

Steps are in [Compare clearances on a fit test plate](/help/compare-fit-clearances).
