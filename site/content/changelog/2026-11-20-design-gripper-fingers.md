You can make editable flat or V-shaped gripper fingers around a selected object, then test its grasp in a Case. In the desktop app, you can use the declared two-M3 sliding-carriage interface with rigid boxes or circular cylinders.

Finger length, thickness, clearance and mounting dimensions stay linked across the pair. Each pair includes four M3 × 16 screws, four washers and four captive nuts.

A narrow cylinder needs a different gripping face from a carton. In the ordinary pickup Case, you got a block sized to the gripper's opening. With **Test Grasp**, you keep the selected object's exact dimensions, supplied weight and declared friction. The existing reference approaches, grips, lifts, places and releases that target.

To pass a try, you need recorded contact with both jaws and retention until placement. Pushing without lifting, palm contact alone and dropping the object before placement fail. You get simulated evidence from the result, which doesn’t establish physical grasp performance. If you use an unsupported mount, obstruct the fasteners or enter impossible dimensions, Basecase refuses the operation before changing the scene.

You can send generated fingers to the ordinary Print and Kit outputs with the same part identities. Follow the steps in [Design gripper fingers](/help/design-gripper-fingers).
