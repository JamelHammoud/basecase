You can distribute a starter robot and grade a class of submitted designs in the browser. You and your students exchange downloaded files without accounts.

Open a Case, set its budget and choose **Export Assignment** from the command palette. Students import that file through **Classroom**, edit their own scene and choose **Submit**.

You can give the class the same cup-carrying task under $60, and each student can change the robot’s shape, joints or motors. Supply the original assignment when you grade so students can’t replace its rules with submitted Cases, budgets or claimed scores.

To grade submissions, run the Case reference across 20 prescribed seeds and recompute engineering Checks, estimated cost and mass. You evaluate each design through the reference result, not the student’s learned controller. Open any tile to inspect an independent robot scene or a seeded replay.

You can keep 30 submissions and 64 MB of submission files in a workspace, with at most 8 MB per file. Use fingerprints to detect mismatches, but you can’t authenticate authorship with them. You grade within your browser, and grading doesn’t grant Ready to build.

Steps are in [Grade a class](/help/grade-a-class).
