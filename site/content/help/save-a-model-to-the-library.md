Keep a part or a whole design in the Library so you can drop it into any scene later.

1. Select the parts you want to keep.
2. Click Library in the view rail on the left.
3. Click + at the top right of the Library.
4. Choose **Save Selection**, or **Save Whole Scene** to keep everything.
5. Click the saved model, or drag it onto the canvas, to insert it in another scene.

![The New Model menu open at the top right of the Library](/help/save-a-model-to-the-library/new-model-menu.png)

The same menu has **Import SVG**, which extrudes a drawing into a part when you click **Add**, and **From Image**, which builds a model from a picture. Keep an SVG part here with **Save to Library**, the bookmark in its Inspector header.

![The Import SVG panel with a drawing loaded and one Add button under Output](/help/save-a-model-to-the-library/import-svg.png)

Hover a model to see where it came from, its size and how many parts it has. Right-click it to rename, duplicate or delete it.

![A model's details beside the Library](/help/save-a-model-to-the-library/model-details.png)
