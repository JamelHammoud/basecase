You can choose cable stock and route a robot's cables through points attached to its links. Open **Edit Route** from a **Cable reach** finding or a connection under **Wiring** in **Build**.

You may need more cable as an arm's wrist extends. Choose the stock you plan to use, place points on the links that hold the cable, and read the recomputed check. You enter point coordinates in millimeters. Drag a point to preview its move before adding an undo step.

You'll see the chosen stock in the bill of materials. In Build Along, you keep the modeled rest route and cable identity. Click **Show Pose** to open the measured limiting joint position in **Pose**.

You still get the existing check's conservative clearances and motion limits. You can’t use it to certify physical bend radius or whole-robot readiness.

Steps are in [Route a robot cable](/help/route-a-robot-cable).
