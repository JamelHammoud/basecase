You can keep assembly photos beside each saved robot's Build Along steps in **Build** ▸ **Builds**.

Two arms made from the same Kit can have different bracket installations. You keep their photos with the build and saved step they belong to. A thumbnail opens a larger image beside the expected CAD assembly.

You can attach up to 20 different PNG or JPEG photos to each step. Photos with identical bytes share storage across builds, and each attachment keeps its own import time. You can reopen a build, reverse completion or edit the source design without changing which build and step a photo belongs to.

Each file must fit within 20 MB and 16 megapixels, with at most 8,192 pixels on either edge. You have a separate 256 MB allowance for photos and a limit of 2,000 attachments on this device. Basecase asks you to confirm before deleting a photo or build, and keeps shared image content while another attachment uses it.

Photos don't establish assembly correctness or change Checks, materials or movement permissions. Follow the steps in [Keep assembly photos with a robot build](/help/keep-assembly-photos).
