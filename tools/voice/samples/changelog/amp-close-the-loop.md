Close the Loop - Amp

		                   You can now send feedback to Amp directly from your website.

Enable the feedback widget in the settings for your project and add one script to your website.

Then visit the site with ?amp appended, and the annotation widget is displayed.

Click on any part of the page and write instructions for Amp.

Once you are done, send the feedback to Amp. The first time, you sign in with Amp. Then Amp starts a new thread in your project and makes the change.

You ship it from the thread like any other change.

Use It

By default the widget is invisible: it only shows up when you open the site with ?amp, or when you have used the widget before.

Click the widget, then click an element on the page and write what you want changed. Click New Comment to comment on another one.

When you're done, click Send. The first time, Amp asks you to connect your account: click Continue with Amp, sign in, and approve the consent screen.

Then click Start in Orb. Amp starts a new thread in your project with your comments, and Open Thread takes you there.

After that, the widget shows up on this site without ?amp, until you hide it.

Read more about the feedback widget in the docs.
