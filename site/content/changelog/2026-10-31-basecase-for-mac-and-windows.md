---
title: Basecase for Mac and Windows
date: 2026-10-31
version: 0.1.0
---

Basecase 0.1.0 is out for Apple silicon Macs and 64-bit Windows 11. You design a robot in 3D, and an agent works in the same scene with you.

The agent is Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI, signed in with your own account. Basecase downloads the one you pick the first time you use it. Offline, the built-in outline tracer, needs no account.

The app checks for a new version at launch and every hour, and downloads it in the background. Nothing installs until you click the update button in the view rail.

The same studio runs in a browser at [app.basecase.so](https://app.basecase.so), with no download, and saves scenes in the browser. The agent, printing, SimReady models and training need the desktop app. The desktop app isn't built for Intel Macs.

To pick an agent, follow [Choose the agent](/help/choose-the-agent).
