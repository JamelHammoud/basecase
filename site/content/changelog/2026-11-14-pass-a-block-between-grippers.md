---
title: Pass a block between grippers
date: 2026-11-14
image: /changelog/pass-a-block-between-grippers/cover.png
---

You can now hand a loose block from one gripper to another and place it on a floor spot. The **Hand Off to a Spot** goal runs on two arms opened in separate scene tabs.

Previously, each arm only had to reach the same point in turn. A passed try could leave both hands empty. The new task requires the donor to lift the block, both grippers to hold it together, then the recipient to carry it alone.

![Both grippers holding the block during transfer](/changelog/pass-a-block-between-grippers/cover.png)

Recordings keep the block's motion and both jaws' contacts, sampled 50 times a second. A close command alone can’t prove a grip.

Both arms must have grippers that fit the block and can reach the transfer. Older saved Cases keep their point-touch task.

Steps are in [Put two robots in one Case](/help/put-two-robots-in-one-case).
