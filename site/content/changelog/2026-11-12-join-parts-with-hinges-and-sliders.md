---
title: Join parts with hinges and sliders
date: 2026-11-12
---

Select two parts and choose Add Hinge or Add Slider from the right-click menu or ⌘K, then click a round face to set the axis, so a model you drew elsewhere can be posed and simulated. When one part's pin already sits in the other's hole, the joint lines up on its own and the Pose tab opens on it.
