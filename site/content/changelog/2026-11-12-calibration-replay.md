---
title: Calibrate servos in Operate
date: 2026-11-12
---

Calibrate in Test moves each emulated joint through a bench sequence and fits its servo, then lists a verdict and version per joint under Calibration, with Roll Back in the row's More menu. Clicking a joint replays its bench log through the sim with the preset and with the calibration, drawn over what the servo really did, with the median error of each. Pick an older version from the More menu to see what rolling back would change before you do it.
