You can now right-click a broken part and choose Repair to follow its teardown, replacement, and reassembly in Build Along.

A cracked wrist bracket needs a replacement even when its shape has stayed the same. Repair clears that part's printed status while keeping the rest of the print history.

The walkthrough removes the smallest set of parts that the measured paths and proven removal order allow. The replacement callout uses the build guide's item name. For a bought servo, that is the catalog item from the bill of materials.

At a printed part's replacement step, Print opens a plate with one copy of that part. Saved skip and copy settings don’t add parts to the repair plate. Sending the print still starts with your click.

Reassembly follows the build guide's order for the removed parts. Repair can’t produce a walkthrough when access or reassembly is unproven.

Steps are in [Repair a part](/help/repair-a-part).
