Bring a model from another app into the scene as a printable part. Basecase reads STL, 3MF, OBJ, PLY, FBX, GLB, glTF and DAE files.

1. Right-click an empty spot on the canvas.
2. Choose **Import Mesh**.
3. Pick one or more files.

Each file becomes its own part, selected and ready to move. You can also drop the files on the canvas, or press ⌘K and choose **Import Mesh**.
