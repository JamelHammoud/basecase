# src/linkage/

Linkages from a path: Sketch's Path mode and the agent's `synthesize_linkage` fit a four-bar to drawn points (`src/utils/linkage.ts`, in a worker) and insert it as printed bars with a servo on the crank. Before editing here, read:

- [Inspector and selection](../../docs/guide/inspector-and-selection.md) (§8), the Sketch bullet
- [Joints and motion](../../docs/guide/joints-and-motion.md) (§8c), the Linkages bullet
- [Agent](../../docs/guide/agent.md) (§9)

The rules in the root [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) apply here too. When you change how this area works, update its guide in the same change.
