# Tracking testing evidence

Coverage: [Cases](../guide/cases.md#testing-coverage) and [Cameras](../guide/cameras.md#testing-coverage). Placement: [Vision evidence index](testing-vision.md). Shared contracts: [Running and testing](../guide/running-and-testing.md#0e-tests).

## Tracking, zones and lines

Coverage for object tracking and counting, added with the tracker in `src/vision/tracking/`.

- `vision/tracking/tracker.test.ts` replays twelve scripted workflows from `fixtures/workflows.ts` through the pure tracker and checks crossings, zone peaks, final zone counts, dwell and id sets: conveyor part counting, doorway entry and exit, bin occupancy, arm pick-zone occupancy, rover lane blocking, two crossing people, a briefly occluded object, an object that leaves and returns, a drawer past a line, detector flicker, nothing crosses and a detection of the wrong class. It also covers id reassignment limits, the track, detection and camera caps, restart when time runs backwards, regions that change, two cameras with the same zone and line names, and a removed camera.
- `routines/visionTracking.test.ts` replays the same workflows through real ONNX WASM inference and `trackResult`, reads crossings and zones as the signals a console, a Case contract and a Routine read, repeats the conveyor on a source that delivers every second frame, keeps two cameras with identical names apart, and feeds the results to a Case on a MuJoCo `SimEngine`.
- `cases/console/regions.test.ts` covers zones and lines surviving save and reopen, two cameras with the same names, naming new drawings, refusals and the signal names. `cameras/visionRegions.test.ts` covers regions reaching the worker once per change and restarting tracking, and `cameras/visionSignals.test.ts` covers tracking signals, zeroing when nothing is left and the newest model supplying a camera's zones.
- `e2e/visionTracking.e2e.test.ts` opens a Case with a simulated camera, draws a line on its tile with **Draw Line**, loads the scripted ONNX detector through `__studio.loadVisionModel`, feeds the conveyor frames through the worker with `__studio.feedVisionFrames`, and reads four crossings in the console and on the tile. It does not depend on the rendered-camera frames of the walkthrough quarantined under [#992](https://github.com/JamelHammoud/basecase/issues/992): frames come from the scripted workflow, so the declaration is neither restored nor edited. A second story reopens a saved Case with its zone and line drawn.

The scripted frames are 2 × 2 images whose twelve color values encode up to four box centres and a presence flag, so a frame's boxes come from real model output and not a mock. The frame alpha is opaque, since a transparent pixel loses its color when the browser makes a bitmap. Not covered: a trained detector's own flicker and misses on real footage, an operator camera feed with a live person, and the onboard runtime. Timings and memory are in [Vision inference performance](performance-vision-inference.md#tracking-runs-in-the-inference-worker-at-a-fixed-small-cost).
