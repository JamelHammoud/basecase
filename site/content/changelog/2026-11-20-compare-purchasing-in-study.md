You can compare supplier evidence for robot alternatives in Study, beside each design's engineering checks and Case results. The comparison uses a required component arrival date, destination and robot quantity.

A shoulder motor can meet the robot's torque requirements and arrive too late. In Study, you compare the actual bill of materials for each supported alternative, including servo versions, batteries and named dimensions.

In each row, you can see the reasons from confirmed supplier offers, the contributing lines and source references. Demand accounts for available owned stock, packaging and price breaks. You see separate totals for each currency. Each design's supplier offers open with its bill of materials and saved arrival requirements.

The scene retains the evidence snapshot and evaluation time, so you keep the historical comparison when you reopen it. Apply rechecks current evidence and refuses inputs that have changed or expired since the comparison. You apply a candidate as one undoable design edit.

Quoted arrival covers components. It excludes print time, assembly and completed-robot delivery. Complete purchasing evidence doesn’t establish Ready to build or a passing Case.

Steps are in [Compare purchasing in Study](/help/compare-purchasing-in-study).
