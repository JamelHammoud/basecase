The first time you open Basecase, a two-wheel rover is already in the simulator. It rolls toward a blue spot on its own, and you can take over with the keyboard.

1. Open Basecase. The first tab is Reach a Spot: Two-wheel rover, and the Simulate tab is open on the right.
2. Watch the rover drive about 90 cm to the spot and stop on it.
3. Click the canvas, then hold [[W]] to drive forward, [[S]] to back up, and [[A]] or [[D]] to turn. The first key you press takes over from the rover's own route.
4. To put the rover back at the start, press [[R]]. To stop driving, press [[Esc]].
5. To see the rover's chassis and three wheels, open Layers in the view rail.

![The rover on its way to the spot, with Simulate open on the right](/help/drive-your-first-robot/first-launch.png)

Basecase saves nothing until you change the scene. If you close the scene without changing anything, Basecase opens the rover again the next time you open the app. After you save any scene, Basecase opens your scenes instead. To start an empty canvas at any time, press [[⌘T]].
