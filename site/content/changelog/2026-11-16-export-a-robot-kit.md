You can export a robot's plates, sheet cut files, assembly instructions and packing list together through **Export** ▸ **Kit**, on desktop and web.

If you own half the screws, you need different quantities for shopping and assembly bags. In the kit, you can see total demand, owned stock and remaining purchases as separate quantities. You keep part identities across repeated modules and extra printed copies outside the assembly steps.

In the archive, you get an illustrated HTML guide, per-material 3MF plates, sheet DXF files, source records and the Robot Package joint map. You keep exact catalog variants and bus identities in the packing and servo records.

To export, you need movable joints with exact actuator identities. You can include up to 256 MB of contents before compression. You keep each check's measurement time and freshness. Exporting doesn’t mark parts purchased, assembly complete or the design ready to build.

Steps are in [Export a robot kit](/help/export-a-robot-kit).
