# src/datasets/

Datasets of tries from any scene, versioned (`datasetFile.ts`, kept by `datasetStore/`), exported as LeRobot v3.0 for `lerobot-train` and v2.1 for GR00T, camera views rendered and joined in a worker. Before editing here, read:

- [Cases](../../docs/guide/cases.md) (§8h, Datasets)
- [Performance](../../docs/guide/performance.md) (§0f)

The rules in the root [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) apply here too. When you change how this area works, update its guide in the same change.
