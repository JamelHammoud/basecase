You can compare shared robots on a pinned Case, replay their measured seeds in the browser, and open either robot in the studio.

Enter League publishes an immutable robot revision from the Share Robot sheet or the command palette. Updating a shared link can’t change an accepted entry. Submission requires the desktop app and a Hugging Face account. The League publisher must record a trusted receipt before the deadline.

Rover sprint compares a two-wheel body with a four-wheel body on one Case. Both miss the spot in the shipped runs, but their contract margins differ, so you can inspect each run before changing the design.

In the standings, entries rank by passes, then the declared score direction, then lower Budget. A stable identity breaks remaining ties. Budget stays unpriced when prices or geometry are missing.

After the deadline, the publisher’s adjudication workflow freezes the accepted entries and generates held-back seeds before replay. It uses the League’s pinned runner commit and publishes complete standings after every entry finishes. Browser replays stay provisional.

You can’t enter Leagues with executable Case panels, Case logic or imported worlds.

Follow the steps in [Replay a League](/help/replay-a-league).
