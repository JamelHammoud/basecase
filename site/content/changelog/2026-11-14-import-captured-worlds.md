You can now drop a captured or generated world onto the canvas and try your robot in it at true size. A World Labs Marble export, a capture from Polycam, Scaniverse or Luma, or an environment mesh opens New Case with the world picked.

The import accepts one GLB, glTF or OBJ collider, with an optional SPZ or PLY splat and any linked files. The splat supplies the view, while separate collision pieces keep doorways and the space under tables open. Without a splat, the world shows its mesh with its own colors or textures.

Ground goals work with the imported world, and you can drive and record tries in it. A splat needs its collider to import. Camera tiles and dataset videos don’t show the imported visual layer.

Steps are in [Import a captured world](/help/import-a-world).
