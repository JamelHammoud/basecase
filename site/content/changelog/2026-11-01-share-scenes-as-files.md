---
title: Share scenes as files
date: 2026-11-01
version: 0.1.1
---

You can now send someone a whole scene as one `.basecase` file, and they open it in their own Basecase.

Before this release, a design left the app as an image, an STL, 3MF plates or a USDZ model. Each keeps the shapes and drops the joints and the comments.

Choose Export ▸ Basecase Scene to save one. To open one, drop it on the canvas, or choose Open Scene File in ⌘K or the File menu. It opens in a tab and saves to the Library.

The file carries the parts, imported meshes included, the comments and the scene's goal. The chat, print history, trained policies and the build guide stay on your computer. A file from a newer Basecase asks you to update before it opens.

Steps are in [Share a scene](/help/share-a-scene) and [Open a shared scene](/help/open-a-shared-scene).
