You can make up to twenty individual builds from one frozen Kit. Open **Build** ▸ **Builds** to reserve owned components and assign printed copies to each robot.

Two rovers can share a shopping list, but they can’t share the same physical motor. Reserve a motor for Rover 1 and you can’t allocate it to Rover 2. You reserve physical pieces, even if you buy them in packs. Choosing an offer adds no stock.

Each build keeps its original geometry, name and assembly progress. In the print history, you keep the declared copy count from each attempt. From a completed two-copy plate, you can assign one copy to each rover and save each rover’s ownership of its copy.

You can correct stock without changing existing owners and see any shortages. You must confirm returns. Deleting a build releases no materials.

Before you can allocate copies from legacy print records, you must reconcile their quantities. You can’t allocate copies with unverifiable source mappings.

You can use these records to track software accounting. You still need to inspect the physical parts and check their dimensional fit. Allocating parts or completing a build leaves the Checks verdict unchanged.

Steps are in [Allocate parts to robot builds](/help/allocate-parts-to-robot-builds).
