title: Prepare prints with current supported profiles
date: 2026-11-28
image: /changelog/refresh-printer-profiles/cover.png
---

You can now choose from 97 supported printers with 0.4 mm nozzle presets in Settings. We refreshed the profiles using Bambu Studio 2.9.2 and OrcaSlicer 2.5.2 sources.

Open a CORE One project in OrcaSlicer and you keep its printer, process and material identities. Install OrcaSlicer 2.5.2 to use its corrected inherited overhang settings. We record the source version in the catalog apart from your installed slicer version.

We exclude configurations with tool assignments Basecase can't represent, including H2D, H3C and P3C. You keep the identities of existing supported selections. For removed models, Basecase keeps the saved names and stops plate preparation instead of substituting another printer. Basecase stops preparation if you lack an installed preset. You can use the CORE One L profiles with PLA alone.

We make no print-quality claim for this refresh. Read [Add a printer](/help/add-a-printer) for printer setup.
