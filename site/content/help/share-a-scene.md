Save a scene as a `.basecase` file to send to someone else.

1. Open the scene in a tab.
2. Click **Export** in the title bar.
3. Choose **Basecase Scene**.
4. Send the downloaded `.basecase` file.

The file holds the parts, comments and the scene's goal. Print history, trained policies and the build guide stay on your computer.
