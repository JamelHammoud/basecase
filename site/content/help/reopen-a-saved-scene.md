You can open a scene you worked on before in a tab. Search for it by its name or goal.

1. Press [[⌘O]] to open the **Scenes** menu.
2. Type part of the scene's name or goal in **Search scenes**.
3. Click the scene.

The scene opens in a new tab. If the front tab is a blank scene you haven't touched, the scene opens there instead. If the scene is already open, Basecase switches to its tab.

You can also open the **Scenes** menu with the button next to the sidebar button in the title bar, or by choosing **File** ▸ **Show Scenes**.

## Rename, duplicate or delete a scene

1. In the **Scenes** menu, right-click the scene.
2. Choose **Rename**, **Duplicate** or **Delete**.

Basecase asks before it deletes a scene, and you can't undo a delete. When you duplicate a scene, Basecase adds “copy” to the name, then “copy 2” for the next duplicate.

To keep several scenes open at once, see [Open, rename and close scene tabs](/help/open-rename-and-close-tabs).
