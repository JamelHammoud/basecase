---
title: Calibrate servos
date: 2026-11-12
---

Calibrate Servos moves each joint of a connected emulated robot through a slow bench run and fits its servo, so the Simulate tab, Cases and training run on the fit. It's in Operate once the robot reaches Test, and in ⌘K; each joint shows its verdict and version, and Roll Back in the row's More menu returns to the one before.

Clicking a joint replays its bench log through the sim with the preset and with the calibration, drawn over what the servo really did, with the median error of each. Pick an older version from the More menu to see what rolling back would change before you do it.
