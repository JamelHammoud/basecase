Build your own robot from your teacher's starter design and submit a portable robot file. You don't need an account.

1. In the browser studio, press [[⌘K]].
2. Choose **Classroom**.
3. In **Assignment file**, select your teacher's `.caseclass` file.
4. In **Name**, enter your display name or pseudonym.
5. Click **Start Attempt**.
6. Edit the robot in the studio.
7. Press [[⌘K]].
8. Choose **Submit**.
9. Send the downloaded `.casesubmit` file to your teacher.

![An assignment loaded in Classroom with Ada entered as the display name](/help/grade-a-class/assignment.png)

![Ada's independent robot scene after adding a cube and exporting a submission](/help/grade-a-class/attempt.png)

The browser saves your edits and assignment context. Your robot file includes the material, infill and power supply settings. If you start a new attempt for the same assignment, you create another scene and keep your earlier attempts.

You can experiment with other Cases. Your teacher grades your submission using the original assignment's Case, budget and reference controller.
